Quick Summary of DA for 9-15 Clifford St, Suffolk Park

DRAFT

Details

  • A 60-person restaurant (+ up to 9 staff)
  • A 30-person cafe (+ up to 3 staff)
  • Total of 40 bedrooms spread over 16 units/houses (mix of 1-4 bedrooms)

The main concerns are:

Parking

  • There will be 35 parking spots/garages for all the shops and dwellings.
  • One 6m wide driveway (like the driveway next to YellowFlower) will be used for 33 spots + delivery trucks.
  • There will be 1 new on-street spot.

Loss of affordable housing

  • There is a NET LOSS of 16 affordable bedrooms.
  • The existing 9 affordable houses will be demolished – a loss 21 bedrooms.
  • The new development has 5 'affordable' bedrooms (over 3 units), and 35 bedrooms (over 13 units/houses) at market rent.
  • NOTE: The definition of affordable in government legislation is that the rent is capped at 80% of market rent for 15 years.

Loss of trees

  • 17 critically endangered littoral rainforest and endangered swamp sclerophyll trees will be removed. These trees can be used by koalas for feed and habitat.

Size and scale

  • Most of the building will be the same height as the top of the childcare centre.
  • Jimmy's Coffee will be replaced by houses.

Traffic and Congestion

  • There are no plans for any road upgrades relating to this DA.
